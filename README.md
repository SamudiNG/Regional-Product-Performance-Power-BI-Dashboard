# Regional & Product Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI Regional & Product Performance Dashboard developed to analyze sales, profitability, product performance, country-level results and customer segment contributions.
The dashboard supports data-driven business decisions by helping users compare performance across countries, products and business segments, while exploring sales trends over time.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Compare sales and profit contributions across countries.
- Identify high-performing products based on sales and profit.
- Analyze customer segment performance.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.
- Support business decisions through interactive performance reporting.

## Key KPIs
- Total Sales - 118.73M
- Total Profit - 16.89M
- Profit Margin % - 14.23%
- Total Units Sold - 1.13M
- Average Sale Price - 118.43
- Average Profit per Unit - 15.01
  
These values reflect the full dataset date range September 1, 2013 to December 1 2014 with all product, country and segment filters set to All. Values are rounded for display.
  

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes the following interactive visualizations,
- Sales by Country- Compares sales contributions across geographic markets.
- Profit by Countr- Evaluates profitability across countries.
- Sales by Product- Identifies products contributing the most sales.
- Profit by Product- Compares product-level profitability.
- Sales by Segment- Shows sales distribution across business segments.
- Profit by Segment- Compares segment-level profit contributions.
- Monthly Sales Trend- Displays changes in sales over time.
- Interactive Filters- Enables analysis by date, product, country and segment.

## Key Insights

1. Overall Sales and Profitability
The dashboard reports approximately 118.73M in total sales and 16.89M in total profit, resulting in an overall profit margin of 14.23%.

2. Product Performance
Paseo generates the highest sales and profit among the products shown in the dashboard. Product-level comparisons help identify which products contribute most strongly to overall business performance.

3. Country-Level Performance
The United States of America, Canada, France, Germany and Mexico contribute to overall sales performance. The country-level charts highlight differences in sales and profitability across markets.

4. Customer Segment Performance
The Government segment is the largest contributor to sales and profit in the dashboard. Segment-level analysis also reveals differences in performance across Small Business, Enterprise, Midmarket and Channel Partners.

5. Monthly Sales Trends
Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the reporting period. The trend visualization helps identify stronger and weaker sales months for further business investigation.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing products- Review the factors supporting strong-performing products such as Paseo and assess opportunities to sustain their              performance.
- Evaluate country-level opportunities- Compare sales and profit contributions across markets to identify areas for growth and improvement.
- Review customer segment profitability- Examine the profitability of each segment, particularly segments with lower profit contributions.
- Investigate monthly sales fluctuations- Explore possible drivers of higher and lower sales periods to support planning and forecasting.
- Monitor profit margins- Track changes in sales and profitability together to support sustainable business growth.
  
## Methodology

- Data Preparation- Prepared the financial sales dataset for analysis in Power BI.
- Data Modeling- Organized the data to support date-based and category-based reporting.
- DAX Measures- Developed measures for total sales, total profit, profit margin, units sold, average sale price and average profit per unit.
- Data Visualization- Created interactive KPI cards, bar charts, a donut chart and a monthly sales trend chart.
- Business Analysis- Compared performance across products, countries and customer segments to identify insights and develop recommendations.

 ## KPI Calculation Notes

- Total Sales- Sum of recorded sales values.
- Total Profit- Sum of recorded profit values.
- Profit Margin %- Total Profit divided by Total Sales.
- Total Units Sold- Sum of units sold.
- Average Sale Price- Arithmetic average of the Sale Price column across records, not a units-sold-weighted average.
- Average Profit Per Unit- Total Profit divided by Total Units Sold.
